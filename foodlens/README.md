# 🍲 FoodLens

Snap a photo of Nigerian food → know what it is + how much is too much.

## 🎯 The Problem
Many Nigerians eat foods that can harm them in excess — high carbs,
high oil, high sodium. But there's no easy way to know what's on your
plate and what it does to your body.

## 💡 The Solution
Upload a photo of your food. The app:
1. Identifies the dish (ML model)
2. Shows health warnings (curated database)
3. Tells you who should avoid it

## 🍽️ Supported Dishes (v1)
- Jollof Rice
- Egusi Soup
- Pounded Yam
- Suya
- Fried Plantain

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- PyTorch (image classification)
- Streamlit (web app)
- Python
